- [SECURITY] Verified TLS, HTTP(S)/DNS/redirect policy and pinned direct connections.

- Preserve hidden rows/logos on filtered edits; retain the existing playlist after failed imports.

- Unified environment/version configuration, automatic cross-platform setup and packaged CLI/assets.
- Mandatory offline tests/lint/build in CI; updated setup, usage and security documentation.
